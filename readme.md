**Track real Amazon price history while you browse.**

TruePrice is a lightweight Chrome/Brave/Edge extension that captures product prices and turns them into a clear, readable history—so you can make decisions based on real data, not vibes.

---

## Why TruePrice

* **Instant context**
  See where today’s price sits compared to recent highs and lows.

* **Clean visuals**
  Hover over any point to view the exact date and price.
